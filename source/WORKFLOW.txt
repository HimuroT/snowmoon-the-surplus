ACTUAL PRODUCTION WORKFLOW AND REBUILD MAP
The Surplus — 84-second public source edition

1. Scene planning
The prepared shotlist.json and the-surplus.en.srt define eleven story beats
and seventeen caption cues at 24 fps. The creative brief and selected actual
instruction excerpts describe the intended performance and visual direction.

2. Original vector construction
character_rig.py defines the complete characters and street geometry as SVG
paths. The rig includes three-quarter faces, eyes, brows, mouths, head/torso
transforms, jointed arms, supporting/swinging legs, foot lift and shadows.
There are no downloaded illustration assets, textures or character sheets.

3. Scene staging and frame generation
animate_film.py applies shot-specific poses, framing, reflection masks,
caption placement, skyline geometry, timing and end-card text. The earlier
refined 66–78s staging is retained without its proof label. For each frame,
the program writes one editable SVG and renders it using installed Inkscape.
  python3 source/animate_film.py --workers 6
All 2,016 generated SVGs are included and checked against the source function.

4. Procedural audio
synthesize_audio.py uses fixed random seeds, filtered noise and oscillator
partials to synthesize the wind, street wash, rotor, cloth, two sets of shoes
and sparse wood-like musical notes. It includes the procedural reference
needed to preserve the refined excerpt without any earlier-folder dependency.
  python3 source/synthesize_audio.py
Outputs are 48kHz stereo float32 WAVs: mix, ambience, effects and music.

5. Video assembly
The supplied render_film.sh calls FFmpeg with the rendered PNG sequence,
original mix, H.264 CRF18, yuv420p and stereo AAC 192k. It sets 24 fps and 84s,
then extracts the representative still at 71.5s. The complete exact commands
are in that script. No remote service is called by the rebuild.

6. Verification
FFprobe inspects both streams and duration. FFmpeg decodes the complete
video, emits frame hashes and decodes the audio for numeric peak checks.
verify_and_package.py checks every frame source, every caption boundary,
expected dark fades, full music silence 70–79.7s and archive integrity.
Representative shot and gait frames were visually inspected. Realtime
synchronized playback and perceptual listening remain unverified.

7. Distribution packaging
verify_and_package.py can create a complete source archive with float32
masters. package_delivery.py creates ordinary independent small ZIPs and
full-length PCM24 FLAC audio copies. It measures conversion error against
float32 and includes the result. Audio files are not time-segmented; no
multipart-archive joining is used. Full float32 is regenerated by step 4.

Public URLs and publication tools are outside this creative/rebuild pipeline.
The released audiovisual content is unchanged from the reviewed export; only
container metadata, distribution names and documentation were prepared for
public use. Rebuilds use the public output names and metadata in render_film.sh.
